    How a KenMover move works

    White glove, step by step

    Four stops from your first call to your first night in the new place.

    1. 1

      Before move day

      Walkthrough & written flat rate

      A short call or a free in-home walkthrough. You tell us what's moving, we write it down, and your flat rate is based on exactly that list.

    2. 2

      Pack day

      Pack & protect

      Every piece gets blanket-wrapped and shrink-wrapped before it moves. Floors, railings and door frames get covered. Boxes are labeled by room.

    3. 3

      Move day

      Load & drive

      Heavy pieces go in first. The truck is loaded tier by tier and strapped floor to ceiling, so nothing shifts on the road.

    4. 4

      Same day

      Set up & settle in

      Beds rebuilt, furniture placed where you point, boxes unpacked if you chose it, and packing debris hauled away.

    Questions

    Straight answers

    Still unsure? Call 682-477-9116 and ask a person.

    Why is my estimate a range?

    It's based on what you tell us online. After a short call or walkthrough we send a written flat rate, and it only changes if the inventory changes.

    What does white glove include?

    Blanket-wrapping and shrink-wrapping, floor and door-frame protection, furniture disassembly and reassembly, careful handling of TVs, art and mirrors, room-by-room placement, and debris removal. Packing and unpacking are optional add-ons.

    How can I keep my move on budget?

    Pick a Tuesday to Thursday in the middle of the month. Tell us about stairs up front, have boxes in one room, and let us know what isn't coming. Every item you leave behind lowers the number.

    Do you charge a travel fee?

    No travel fee within 5 miles of your pickup address.

    Are you licensed and insured?

    Yes. KenMover is registered with the U.S. Department of Transportation (USDOT 3523011), fully insured, BBB Accredited, and a member of the Household Goods Arbitration Program.

    Do you move out of state?

    Yes, we're licensed for interstate moves. Call us and we'll quote it personally.
